The CANSAT Team of SAM

CANSAT is a robotics competition for Austrian schools in which students design and build a small satellite that is the size of a can. The satellite is then launched 700 meters into the air, where it has to carry out certain measurements, like air pressure and temperature.
This year, our students from year two are participating in it.
